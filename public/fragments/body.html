what is this site about?

Looking for somewhere to set up an online portfolio? Annoyed with the mainstream, personality-scrubbed, non-customizable sites that seemed like the only option? Then you've come to the right spot!

chimera-portfolio is your new digital business card: a simple, static, 100% free website template that YOU can download, customize, and make your own.

Built with nothing but HTML, CSS, and a dash of JavaScript, it's a perfect starting point for beginners to web design, and more customizable than any drag-and-drop website creator will ever be. Ever wanted to have a personal website that was truly yours? This is it.


wait, really?

It's true! I've done the heavy lifting of setting up the basic framework so you can stylize it however you see fit. And, the more you want to get out of this template, the more you'll be able to do. Even just a little bit of extra knowledge about HTML and CSS will get you a long way!

With this template, you can express your vision to your heart's content. Keep it simple and put in your own text, colors, images, and info, or go all-out and build whole new custom pages and formatting. The only limit is your imagination!
