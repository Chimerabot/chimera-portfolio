about me

I'm Gideon (but I go by chimerabot in most places online). As of the time of writing, I'm a second-year computer science student at Colorado State University.

Currently, I work with the Sustain lab here at CSU on a couple of projects involving geospatial data, including a cool web app called SoilTwin. I do most of its UI design, working on features that let users animate data on a map across time and build queries that show the intersections between different datasets.

Some of my hobbies include cooking, climbing, writing, gamedev, and playing the trombone. I've always loved working with computers, though, and this template is no exception!

You can reach me on GitHub @Chimerabot, or by email at gideon@lo-fi.net.
